The site positions Mid South as a specialty-lubricant supplier for food-processing, industrial, and manufacturing operations. Its recurring themes are food-safety compliance, longer equipment life, reduced downtime, performance under heat/cold/pressure, technical guidance, and personal service.

Homepage language includes:

- “Engineered for Food Safety. Built to perform in the toughest environments.”
- High-performance lubricants for food-grade compliance, industrial durability, and reduced downtime.
- Custom solutions for food, industrial, and manufacturing sectors.
- Products for high-temperature bearings, freezers, chains/conveyors, hydraulic systems, compressors, ammonia refrigeration, and heat-transfer systems.

The site says its customers range from regional food processors to national manufacturing plants, but it does not name a verified customer.

## Compliance and performance claims present on product records

Claims vary by product and should be verified before reuse. The catalog includes references to:

- H1 incidental-food-contact requirements and FDA 21 CFR 178.3570
- NSF-approved or NSF H1 formulations
- NSF HT-1 registration for food-grade heat-transfer fluids
- Fire-resistant hydraulic-fluid formulations
- Biodegradable and zinc-free hydraulic fluid
- Oxidation stability, rust/corrosion protection, deposit/varnish control, wear protection, water separation, low volatility, and film strength
- Extended compressor drain intervals of 6,000, 8,000, or 10,000 hours depending on product and operating conditions
- High- and low-temperature service, including published maximum temperatures on several heat-transfer products

Use the wording attached to each product rather than applying a compliance claim across the whole catalog.
