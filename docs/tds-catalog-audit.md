## Important corrections

- MSL-2082 is Synthetic Food Grade Gear Fluid, not Food-Grade High-Temperature Gear Lubricant. The unsupported high-temperature emphasis and inferred High Temperature filter were removed.
- MSL-2090 is Base Oil, not Food-Grade White Oil. Its TDS properties table lists Grades 4, 10, 22, 32, 46, 68, and 100, and separately identifies MSL-2090-20 as approved for direct food contact. This designation is not generalized to all grades.
- The former 2585-68 listing now uses MSL-2585 and the TDS's FM Approved Fire Resistant EAL Hydraulic Fluid heading. The TDS grade is ISO 68.
- The former Blue Star 9600/40 listing now uses the authoritative supplied TDS designation BLUE STAR XH 9650/460-1.5 and downloads that sheet. This is a catalog correction based on the user's source-of-truth instruction, not a claim that the two historical designations are interchangeable.
- MSL-6243 TDS grades are 32, 46, 68, and 100; the unsupported Grade 22 was removed.
- MSL-6321 TDS grades are 32, 46, and 68; Grade 68 was added.
- MSL-6488 TDS grades are 32 and 220.
- MSL-6831 TDS grades are 15, 22, and 32, not only the Grade 15 covered by its supplied SDS.

## Products without a supplied TDS

MSL-2284, FRH-46, MSL-6061, and MSL-C12 do not have a supplied TDS. Their existing codes and names are unchanged and remain unverified against TDSs. They retain Request TDS until matching sheets are supplied. No other product's TDS is substituted.

## Other supplied TDSs

MSL-2185 (Synthetic Food Grade Chain Lubricant with Tackifier), MSL-2245 (Semi-Synthetic Food Grade AW Hydraulic), MSL-C13 (Heat Transfer Oil), MSL-Rescue HTF HD (Heavy-Duty Heat Transfer Cleaner Concentrate), MSL-RU865 HD Grease, and SNFG Series of Synthetic Food Grade Oils are distinct products outside the approved 28-product catalog. Their sheets were reviewed but not assigned to other catalog products. Duplicate copies of the MSL-2090 and MSL-2185 TDSs do not represent additional products.

## Preserved behavior

- All SDS actions remain request-only; SDS source mappings were not changed.
- The 60 supplied package SKUs remain unchanged and separate from canonical TDS base codes.
- Existing product IDs and URLs remain stable, including URLs containing former names or codes, to preserve links.
- The catalog, document search, quote/request links, structured product data, and TDS resource library share the corrected catalog identities.
